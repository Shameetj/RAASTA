# Pitch Deck Slide Outline (Developer 4)

## Slide 1: Title & Tagline
- **RAASTA**: Accessibility-Aware Navigation for Wheelchair & Deaf Pedestrians.
- Team members & roles.

## Slide 2: The Problem
- Traditional navigation apps focus on vehicles and speed.
- Stairs, broken curbs, blocked ramps, and audio-only navigation leave millions stranded.

## Slide 3: The Solution
- Proactive obstacle collision detection.w
- Certified step-free detour routing with ramp waypoints.
- Multi-sensory visual and tactile vibration cues.
- Comprehensive accessibility score rating.

## Slide 4: Target Profiles
- **Wheelchair Profile**: Step-free navigation, ramp verification, slope ADA thresholds.
- **Deaf / Hard of Hearing Profile**: Visual-first cues, high-contrast hazard alerts, device vibration patterns.

## Slide 5: Architecture & Tech Stack
- Frontend: React + Leaflet + OpenStreetMap.
- Backend: Python + FastAPI + SQLite.
