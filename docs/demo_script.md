# Hackathon Live Demo Script (Developer 4)

## Timing: 2–3 Minutes

### 1. Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
- "Imagine arriving in an unfamiliar city in a wheelchair, trusting a standard map, only to find an unnavigable 18-step flight of stairs with zero ramp in sight. Or being deaf and missing audio turn cues. This is why we built RAASTA."

### 2. Live Demo Part 1: Wheelchair Mode (0:30 - 1:30)
- Click **Metro Station ➔ University Library**.
- Point out the red dashed direct route and the detected stairs marker.
- Show the automatic green accessible detour with verified 1:12 ramp corridor.
- Highlight the **RAASTA Accessibility Score** (88/100) and the slope/ADA breakdown.

### 3. Live Demo Part 2: Deaf / Hard of Hearing Mode (1:30 - 2:15)
- Toggle to **Deaf / Hard of Hearing** mode.
- Demonstrate the high-contrast visual alert banner and the tactile vibration feedback.
- Walk through the visual turn-by-turn cards with tactile ground cues and hazard icons.

### 4. Live Demo Part 3: Crowdsourced Reporting & Closing (2:15 - 2:45)
- Open "Report Blockage" and demonstrate community-driven obstacle reporting.
- "RAASTA makes every journey safe, barrier-free, and inclusive for everyone."
